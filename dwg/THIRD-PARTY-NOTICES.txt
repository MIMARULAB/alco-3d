The files in dwg/fw/ are the DWG converter of the Documents tab, built from viewer-src/tools/dwgwasm/ (Program.cs, dwgconv.csproj, build.sh).

They contain:
- ACadSharp 3.8.35, MIT licence: https://github.com/DomCR/ACadSharp/blob/master/LICENSE
- The .NET 10 runtime and base class libraries for WebAssembly, MIT licence: https://github.com/dotnet/runtime/blob/main/LICENSE.TXT

The full licence texts are at the links above.
